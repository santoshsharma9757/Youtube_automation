# DailyFitX Video Automation - Commands Cheatsheet

## 📱 Short Videos Command
Generate multiple Shorts, perfectly mix your visual footage, and schedule them to upload exactly 1 per day at 8:00 PM:

python main.py --count 5 --long-count 0 --schedule-upload --videos-per-day 1


## 📺 Long Videos Command
Generate multiple Long videos (2-3 minutes), perfectly mix your visual footage, and schedule them to upload exactly 1 per day at 8:00 PM:

python main.py --count 0 --long-count 2 --schedule-upload --videos-per-day 1


## 🤖 THE 5-STEP GOOGLE VEO WORKFLOW

Follow this exact process to create high-end AI Organ Health videos:

**Step 1: Generate the Blueprint & SEO**
Run this command to find a trending topic and generate the prompt + SEO data:
python main.py --count 1 --veo-prompt
*(This saves your text prompt to `output/veo_prompts/` and secretly saves the SEO data to `input/veo_clips/metadata.json`)*

**Step 2: Generate the Video Clips on Google Veo**
Open the generated `.txt` file. Copy the prompt text and paste it into Google Veo.
Wait for Veo to generate the 4 videos, and then download them to your computer.

**Step 3: Staging the Clips**
Move those 4 downloaded videos into your `input/veo_clips/` folder.
CRITICAL: You MUST rename the four files to exactly `1.mp4`, `2.mp4`, `3.mp4`, and `4.mp4` so the script knows the correct order!

**Step 4: Combine & Preview**
Run this command to stitch the videos together so you can review the final result:
python main.py --stitch-veo
*(This saves the combined video to `output/final_videos/` and lets you watch it before uploading)*

**Step 5: Deploy**
If you're happy with the final video, run this command to schedule it to YouTube with SEO and automatically clean up the files:
python main.py --deploy-veo --videos-per-day 1
