Create a high-retention YouTube video concept designed to perform on YouTube Search + Explore in the fitness niche.

Goal:
- Make the video feel viral, practical, and highly clickable.
- Target trending search intent around yoga, gym, exercise, fitness, meditation, and health.
- Build the concept so viewers are likely to stop, watch, save, share, and search for more.

Video sourcing rules:
- Mix local footage and Pexels footage in the same video.
- Use local footage first for authenticity, gym realism, home workout clips, yoga poses, and exercise demos.
- Use Pexels footage to fill gaps with cinematic B-roll, transformations, meditation shots, breathing visuals, running scenes, healthy food prep, and lifestyle health moments.
- Keep visuals fast, dynamic, and pattern-interrupt heavy.
- Prefer portrait framing for Shorts and landscape framing for long-form.

Content rules:
- Topic must come from high-interest YouTube search behavior.
- Prioritize topics like:
  yoga for stress relief, yoga for sleep, gym mistakes, workout mistakes, exercise for belly fat,
  meditation for anxiety, home workout, protein for Indian diet, fat loss myths, posture fixes,
  morning workout, evening workout, breathing exercises, mobility routine, gut health, healthy habits.
- Use a hook style that creates curiosity in the first 2 seconds.
- Favor formats that often go viral:
  mistakes, myth busting, before/after, challenge, comparison, hidden truth, quick fix, beginner guide.
- Make the promise clear and useful, not generic motivation.
- The viewer should feel they learned something immediately valuable.

Output format:
- Give:
  1. Video title
  2. Search keyword
  3. Hook
  4. 5-scene visual plan
  5. Which scenes use Local footage
  6. Which scenes use Pexels footage
  7. CTA

Tone:
- Bold, clean, viral, modern, fitness-focused.
- Avoid fluff.
- Make it feel like a YouTube video that can win both browse traffic and search traffic.
